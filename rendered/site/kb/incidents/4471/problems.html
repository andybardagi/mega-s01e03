The problems

parser: the shared parser reads dates month first

Every tenant in #4471 writes the day first. The shared parser reads every date month first, so a date whose day is 12 or less comes out with day and month swapped: 2 December becomes 12 February. It reaches people through every channel. This is the problem the fix for #4471 targets.

hint: booking-v3 tells people to type the month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People at day-first tenants type what the hint tells them, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, so day and month swap before the parser sees them. It comes only through the form of a booking-v3 tenant. A parser fix does not touch it.

one-day: the date moves by one day

Some reports are no swap at all. A Tuesday shows as Monday, an invite arrives before its booking, two clients in different time zones get different days. The date moved; it did not flip. That points at time zones, not date order, and neither date-order fix reaches it. The desk lists these reports in desk-notes.json and counts them under parser, where they are filed; the table shows them under both.

Why they ended up in one ticket

  • Subject lines. The desk merges on subject alone (decision 0001). Every one of these problems is reported as a wrong date, so they all matched.
  • Intake tags. Intake guesses parser or hint from the reporter’s browser, but the problem depends on the tenant and its form. The tagged as column shows how often the guess is wrong.
